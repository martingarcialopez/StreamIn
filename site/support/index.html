What StreamIn does

Search a movie and StreamIn tells you at a glance whether you can watch it tonight: on one of your streaming services or a free one in your country, or on your services in other countries. Save movies to your watchlist with the heart, and they open complete even offline. StreamIn works on iPhone and iPad, in English and Spanish.

Questions

Where does the data come from?

Movie information comes from The Movie Database (TMDB), and where each movie streams comes from JustWatch, through TMDB. StreamIn isn't endorsed or certified by either.

Why does a movie show nothing, or not the service I expected?

  • It may not be on any of your services, here or abroad. Streaming catalogs change often, and many films are only for rent or purchase, which StreamIn doesn't show.
  • Check that the service is selected in your streaming services, and that your region is the country you're in.
  • StreamIn shows what JustWatch reports, which can lag behind a service's catalog by a day or two.
  • If a row says "Couldn't check availability", pull down to refresh, or check your connection.

How do I change my services or my region?

Open Settings (the gear on the Search or Watchlist tab). Under My subscriptions, tap Streaming services to pick the ones you pay for; turn on Show providers worldwide to find services from other countries. Region sets the country StreamIn checks first, and Language sets the app's language and the movie information (StreamIn restarts to apply it).

Does StreamIn collect my data?

No. There are no accounts, and your settings and watchlist stay on your device. See the privacy policy.
