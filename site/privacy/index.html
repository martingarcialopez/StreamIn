Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 October 2026

In short: StreamIn has no accounts and collects no personal data. Your settings and watchlist stay on your device. There are no analytics, ads or tracking.

No accounts, no personal data

You don't sign up or log in to use StreamIn. The app doesn't ask for your name, email address, location or any other personal information, and the developer receives no data about you or how you use the app. If you've chosen to share analytics with app developers in iOS Settings, Apple may pass on anonymous crash reports and usage statistics.

What stays on your device

StreamIn saves these on your iPhone or iPad only, so the app remembers them between launches:

  • your region and the app's language,
  • the streaming services you pick,
  • your watchlist (the movies you save).

None of this is sent to the developer or synced through iCloud. Deleting the app deletes it.

Searches and movie lookups go to TMDB

To show search results, movie details and where a movie streams, StreamIn requests that information from The Movie Database (TMDB). A request carries what's needed to answer it: the title you searched for or the movie you opened, and your region and language. StreamIn adds no identifier of its own: no account, device or advertising ID. As with any website, TMDB's servers see the network address your request comes from; how TMDB handles that is covered by TMDB's privacy policy.

This means your search history, the titles you search for, is sent to TMDB. It is not linked to you, used for tracking or used for anything beyond answering the search. A future version of StreamIn will keep your recent searches on your device so you can find them again; they will stay on your device, and the searches themselves will still go to TMDB.

Movie posters and streaming service logos are loaded from TMDB's image servers in the same way.

No analytics, ads or tracking

StreamIn contains no analytics, advertising or crash-reporting SDKs, doesn't track you across apps or websites, and doesn't share data with anyone.

Children

StreamIn collects no personal data from anyone, children included.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, this page will be updated and its date will change.
